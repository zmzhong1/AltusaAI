01 · Core workflows

One demo, from intake to completed order.

Follow the operational story from a raw customer list to an attributed, reviewed, and completed warehouse order.

01

Find inventory in context

Search by item, SKU, or barcode and see the result in both the item list and the physical warehouse map.

  • Low-stock and unbinned drilldowns
  • Multi-bin locations with a clear primary bin
  • Live occupancy and recent bin activity
02

Put every item in its place

A guided aisle → section → level picker makes bin IDs understandable and prevents staging locations from becoming default pick bins.

  • Assign, replace, add, and remove locations
  • Recent-bin shortcuts for repeat work
  • Map and inventory KPIs update immediately
03

Turn a list into a warehouse walk

Paste a customer list or use the clearly labeled simulated OCR path. The demo matches catalog items, flags exceptions, and creates a walk-sorted order.

  • Quantities can be reviewed before order creation
  • Skip sends a line to the end of the active walk
  • Short picks create a visible review exception

04

Connect legacy inventory safely

Import a local CSV without sending it anywhere. Rows are validated and previewed before the in-memory catalog changes.

  • Visible header, stock, SKU, and bin validation
  • Duplicate protection and row-level errors
  • Search, map, and KPI updates after import
05

Keep the full order story visible

The order board separates New, Picking, Review, and Completed work while order detail preserves shipping, ownership, edits, notes, refunds, attachments, and timestamps.

  • Picker → Reviewer → Editor attribution
  • Shipping destination, dock, service, and ship-by time
  • Searchable monthly order history

Prototype scope

Clear about what this demo proves.

What you can evaluate

  • The information architecture and primary warehouse workflows
  • Inventory, bins, pick-list intake, order picking, review, editor handoff, and history
  • Keyboard, mobile, and responsive interaction quality
  • A fictional dataset with no dependency on a live backend
Not represented as finished

What is outside this prototype

  • Real authentication, persistence, live collaboration, or audit storage
  • Carrier labels, email, accounting, PDF storage, or ecommerce integrations
  • Real OCR—the image-reading sequence is explicitly simulated
  • Forecasting, AI assistance, real barcode hardware/camera access, or production PWA support
